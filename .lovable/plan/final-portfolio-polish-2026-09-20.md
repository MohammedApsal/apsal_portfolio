# Final portfolio polish

## Goal
Refine the current portfolio without changing its brand, structure, content, portrait, or contact behavior. Make the page tighter, improve portrait integration, and verify the complete experience.

## Changes

1. **Refine the hero portrait**
   - Keep the real portrait and floating labels.
   - Replace the flat tinted-photo feel with a darker cutout-style blend, restrained edge fade, and soft blue/violet backlight while keeping the face natural.

2. **Tighten page rhythm**
   - Reduce oversized section spacing and heading-to-content gaps across the existing sections.
   - Preserve generous readability and the current section order.

3. **Polish project and workflow presentation**
   - Keep all six case studies, unique interface visuals, and existing information.
   - Shorten repetitive problem/solution copy for faster scanning without changing claims.
   - Rework “From Lead to Customer” into the requested compact business-automation path with qualification states and subtle animated connections.

4. **Compact supporting sections**
   - Tighten Business Outcomes, Why Work With Me, How I Work, About, Expertise, Technology, and Contact spacing.
   - Keep the requested personal statement and six-step process prominent but concise.

5. **Improve anchor navigation**
   - Increase the section scroll offset so the fixed navigation never covers section headings.

6. **Validate the finished site**
   - Check desktop, tablet, and mobile for overflow, portrait and mockup rendering, navigation offsets, mobile menu, CTAs, contact links, form behavior, image loading, and runtime errors.

## Technical details
- Changes remain limited to existing React presentation components and shared styling.
- No backend, content model, route, brand, portrait asset, or contact-flow changes.
- Reduced-motion behavior remains supported.
